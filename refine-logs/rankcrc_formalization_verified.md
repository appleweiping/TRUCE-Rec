## Theorem (served-slice selective-risk certificate — frozen-selector marginal expectation)
Let users be exchangeable with label-free features `x_u` and bounded listwise rank-risk `ρ_u = 1 − NDCG@k(u) ∈ [0,1]` (fixed cutoff `k`, frozen base scorer, so each `ρ_u` is fixed before selection). Let the confidence map `g` be fit and **frozen on Fold A**, and assume `g` is **continuous at the operating quantile** (no atom at `λ`; else apply `U(0,ε)` tie-break jitter). Partition the calibration data, disjoint from A, into **B1** and **B2**. On B1 fix the abstention threshold by a PRE-REGISTERED coverage quantile, `λ := Quantile_{1−c}(g over B1)`. On B2 (disjoint from A and B1, hence independent of how `λ` was chosen) compute `m := |{u∈B2 : g(x_u) ≥ λ}|`, `R̂_m := (1/m) Σ_{u∈B2: g≥λ} ρ_u`, and report certified `α := R̂_m·m/(m+1) + B/(m+1)` (B=1). Conditional on the frozen selector `(g, λ)`, the served-B2 users are i.i.d. draws from the fixed conditional law `P(·|g≥λ)`, so the served-B2 mean is an ordinary bounded i.i.d. mean — NOT a self-normalized ratio whose threshold depends on the averaged sample — and the CRC correction applies. Hence for a fresh exchangeable served test user `U*`, **in expectation over the B2 draw and `U*`** (NOT conditional on realized `m`, NOT per-user):
> `E[ 1 − NDCG@k(U*) | g(x_{U*}) ≥ λ ] ≤ α`,  distribution-free + finite-sample.

Assumptions: exchangeability, continuity of `g` at `λ`, frozen-split discipline (`λ` chosen on B1, losses averaged on disjoint B2). Makes NO PAC/high-probability, per-user, or fixed-served-size claim; does NOT integrate over the randomness of fitting `(g,λ)`.

## Key resolved questions
- **`λ ≡ τ`.** For fixed-k NDCG@k over a frozen scorer there is no within-slice risk knob, so the coverage/abstention threshold IS the only risk dial. A distinct `λ≠τ` exists only under a *changed objective*: a charged abstention price `ν` (`R_ν(λ)=E[ρ·1{g≥λ}+ν·1{g<λ}]`), or controlling the unconditional numerator `N(λ)=E[ρ·1{g≥λ}]≤β` with a certified coverage floor `C(λ)≥c0` ⟹ `R=N/C ≤ β/c0`.
- **Why plain CRC does not attach to the naive plug-in.** `R̂(λ)=(1/m)Σ_{g≥λ}ρ` is a self-normalized ratio with random `m`; its per-example contribution is not monotone in `λ`, and if `λ` is chosen on the *same* fold whose losses are averaged, `R̂` is a downward-biased estimate of `E[ρ|g≥λ]` at the data-chosen `λ` (winner's-curse). The disjoint-fold (B1/B2) reduction dissolves this: conditioning on the B1-frozen `λ`, the served-B2 mean is unbiased for the fixed-`λ` conditional risk; verified numerically — CRC slack tracks `(1−θ)/(m+1)` (e.g. +0.028 at E[m]=25 → +0.0008 at E[m]=1000).
- **Monotonicity** of `λ↦R(λ)` is NOT assumed (it is a ratio of two decreasing functions, only population-monotone to the extent `g` orders risk; AUC 0.627, Somers' D ≈ 0.254). Each operating point is its own valid expectation claim. The (risk, coverage) Pareto monotonicity is a POPULATION claim verified over ≥200 resplits; fallbacks if it ever crosses: isotonic (PAV) projection (each point keeps its marginal bound) or fixed-sequence Learn-then-Test (needs only coverage monotonicity, gives a high-probability guarantee).
- **Guarantee class:** marginal-EXPECTATION, conditional on frozen `(g,λ)`. A single deployment may exceed α (frac_held ≈ 0.5 in the safe continuous regime). `(1−δ)` upgrade if needed: fixed-sequence Learn-then-Test (FWER-controlled Hoeffding–Bentkus / betting p-values on the served subsample), valid for a data-chosen threshold and robust to ties, at a coverage cost.
