## Core direction (docs/RESEARCH_IDEA.md)
Uncertainty-aware generative recommendation: when an LLM scores/generates a recommendation, does its uncertainty signal explain *when* the recommendation is reliable — and can we turn that into a **distribution-free risk-controlled reliability guarantee** (a marginal-expectation bound on served-slice rank-risk) rather than just better ranking? This is a recommendation-specific reliability problem, not generic QA calibration, and explicitly NOT a generic LLM reranker (that lane is pony's).

## The contribution — honest dual, headline = RankCRC
TRUCE's contribution is **distribution-free risk-controlled selective reliability**, not raw-ranking SOTA. (Raw same-candidate NDCG@10 on beauty is 0.1143 < the ProEx bar 0.1506 — TRUCE adds a *marginal-expectation guarantee on the served slice*, not raw ranking power. We state this inequality openly.)

**(A) RankCRC (headline, beauty-CPU-VALIDATED) — Risk-Controlled Selective Recommendation.** Given a base ranker + a per-user **label-free** confidence estimator `g`, abstain on low-confidence users so the *served* slice satisfies a conformal-risk-control bound on listwise rank-risk. Four novel pieces:
1. The controlled quantity is **listwise rank-risk** (`1 − NDCG@k`), not pointwise loss (vs standard conformal prediction).
2. The guarantee is on the **served slice after abstention** (selective recommendation), not the full population. **Selection validity (3-fold split discipline):** `g` and the abstention threshold `τ` are fit on a *confidence-fit* fold; the CRC threshold `λ̂` is then tuned on a *separate calibration* fold and applied to test. Conditional on the frozen `(g, τ)`, "served" is a deterministic function of frozen per-user features, so the served users in calibration and test remain exchangeable — CRC applies to the served sub-population without label leakage. The guarantee is thus *marginal over the served slice's calibration+test draw, conditional on a frozen selector*. (Adaptive re-fitting of `g` on the same fold used for `λ̂` would break exchangeability; we explicitly forbid it.)
3. `g` is **label-free** — a logistic model over 24 rank-geometry features (score margins, softmax peakiness, responsibility-entropy order-stats, MC-dropout variance, popularity) with `H_target` FORBIDDEN ⇒ fully deployable (no test-label peek). Beauty: test AUC 0.627 > H_top baseline 0.519.
4. **Selective-Gain Decomposition (Prop 3) — a *falsifiable predictive law*, not a fitted curve.** Under stated assumptions (the per-user NDCG and the confidence score are coupled only through a monotone link; served-slice lift factorizes into a ranking-quality-separation term, a score-dispersion term, and a coverage term), `ΔNDCG@k ≈ (2·AUC − 1)·Gini·κ(c)` predicts the served-slice lift *a priori* from quantities measurable on the calibration fold. It is pre-registered with a **falsification test**: predicted vs observed ΔNDCG across all domains × coverage levels; if the out-of-sample R² is below a pre-set bar the law is demoted to a heuristic in the paper. No coefficients are fit to the test risk — AUC, Gini, κ are all computed on held-out calibration data and plugged in.
**Guarantee type (precise — resolves the central reviewer objection).** RankCRC inherits the **Conformal Risk Control marginal-expectation guarantee** (Angelopoulos–Bates 2024): for an exchangeable calibration+test draw, the tuned threshold `λ̂` satisfies `E[ 1 − NDCG@k | served ] ≤ α`, where the expectation is over the *joint draw of calibration and test*. This is deliberately **NOT** the stronger PAC/high-probability class (`P(R ≤ α) ≥ 1−δ`, RCPS / Bates et al. 2021) and **NOT** a per-user conditional certificate (impossible distribution-free) — we claim exactly the marginal-expectation bound CRC delivers and nothing more. We use "risk-controlled," never "always-holds" or "PAC-certified."
Beauty validation (CPU, paper-labeled) confirms *this* guarantee class, not a different one: the 200-resplit **mean** served-slice risk is ≤ α at all 5 target coverages — i.e. the marginal-expectation bound holds as stated — and the per-split scatter around α (including one 60%-coverage split whose point estimate sits at the bound, within bootstrap CI) is the *expected* behaviour of a marginal (not per-realization) guarantee, **not** a violation. The (risk,coverage) Pareto has 18 non-crossing points. The high-probability variant is offered only as an OPTIONAL add-on (an RCPS-style upper-confidence-bound calibration of `λ̂`) if a domain shows wide per-split spread; the headline claim stays marginal-expectation.

**(B) CALM-Rec (contingent) — Calibrated trust over Attribute-anchored Latent Multi-intent.** Endogenous per-user-item calibrated trust ρ between an LLM multi-intent relevance score and a history-free prior, using intent-mixture geometry (responsibility entropy + ensemble variance) as the reliability signal. **Risk flagged honestly:** on the K=2 CPU proxy the reliability signal is WEAK (responsibility-entropy AUC 0.351 ≈ random; variance AUC 0.576). **Pre-registered contingency:** the GPU beauty Stage-B (real Qwen3-8B encoders, K=4) tests whether the reliability signal is real (Stage-2.5 gate AUC>0.6); if NOT, ρ is demoted to diagnostic and the headline is **RankCRC-only** (selective reliability), keeping the K-intent core with a fixed blend.

## Novelty vs closest work (differentiation, not A+B stitching)
- **Conformal prediction / risk control (Angelopoulos, Bates, et al.):** pointwise loss, full-population coverage. RankCRC controls *listwise rank-risk on the served slice* with a *label-free* confidence — a new controlled quantity + selective framing for recommendation.
- **SelectiveNet / selective classification:** learned abstention with no distribution-free risk guarantee; RankCRC adds the CRC marginal-expectation bound on listwise rank-risk + the falsifiable gain law.
- **LLM-uncertainty calibration (verbalized confidence, ECE):** TRUCE makes NO ECE/reliability-diagram claim; the object is a *served-slice risk-control guarantee*, a different and stronger operational guarantee.

## Feasibility — the 8-domain rollout IS the feasibility proof (not just beauty)
Beauty CPU validation DONE (scripts/rankcrc_validate.py; artifacts committed @design/rankcrc bfc9c94) establishes the mechanism on one domain. A reliability *method* (vs a one-dataset observation) is proven by three cross-domain results, all pre-registered as the feasibility deliverable:
1. **Cross-domain risk control:** the marginal-expectation bound `E[1−NDCG@k|served] ≤ α` holds (mean over resplits ≤ α) on **all 8 domains** at every target coverage — this is what makes it a guarantee, not a beauty artifact.
2. **Useful operating points:** at deployment-relevant α the served coverage is **non-trivial** (we report the coverage–risk Pareto per domain and flag any domain where useful α forces near-zero coverage, rather than hiding it). A method that only serves 5% of users is reported as such.
3. **Label-free confidence transfer:** `g`'s discrimination (AUC) is reported per domain AND under **cross-domain / cross-ranker transfer** (fit `g` on one domain, apply to another). Beauty's AUC 0.627 is modest by design (label-free); the claim is *risk control is maintained wherever `g` ≥ chance*, and the transfer table is the robustness evidence. If `g` collapses to chance on a domain, RankCRC degrades gracefully to "serve-all at the population risk" — stated honestly.

## Kill-argument (strongest reviewer objection) + answerability
"RankCRC is conformal risk control (known) applied to NDCG — incremental; and the raw ranker is below SOTA, so why care?" **Answer:** (i) the *combination* is new — listwise rank-risk + served-slice selective guarantee + a **label-free, deployable** confidence over rank-geometry (no test peek) + a *falsifiable* gain law that *predicts* the lift a priori; (ii) the value is orthogonal to raw SOTA: a risk-controlled "serve only when `E[1−NDCG@k|served]≤α`" is operationally valuable even when raw ranking is mid-pack — it is a *reliability* contribution, positioned as such, not a ranking-SOTA claim; (iii) the empirical novelty that lifts it above "CRC-applied-to-NDCG" is the **8-domain feasibility triad** (cross-domain risk control + non-trivial coverage + label-free confidence transfer) — no prior conformal-recsys work demonstrates a deployable label-free selector holding the bound across 8 domains. If a reviewer still reads it as incremental, the fallback is to foreground the gain-law falsification study + the transfer table as the contribution.
